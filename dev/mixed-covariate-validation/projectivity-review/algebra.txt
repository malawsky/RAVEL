For the current constrained Mendelian covariance model, write V for founder-
scaled visible liability and T* for founder-scaled total liability. A child's
V innovation after .5(Vm+Vp) has variance .5; its T* innovation has covariance
.5 sqrt(1-pi) with that V innovation. Innovations for distinct children are
independent. Parent T* projections use only Vm,Vp, because offspring V
innovations are uncorrelated with parental liabilities.

Let Rp* = Cov(T*parents,Vparents) Var(Vparents)^(-1). Then

  E_linear(T*i | V_all) = .5 1' Rp* Vparents
                         + sqrt(1-pi)[Vi-.5(Vm+Vp)].

Divide the parental rows by sqrt(Vp) and child rows by sqrt(Vc) for the
present-day role-standardized R used in the fitter. No other child's V has
a coefficient. Therefore embedded rows/columns of R agree across family
sizes, and R D has the same common marginal total-covariate associations for
arbitrary free exchangeable D. This does not require imposing the additional
constraint D_child,sibling=.5(D_mother+D_father).

check.R compares this independent algebra with the implemented R and embedded
R D blocks for 1--6 children, all three constrained modes, pi 0/.4/.8 and rho
-.2/0/.3. D has distinct free role coefficients, including a sibling coefficient
that deliberately violates the extra Mendelian-covariate constraint.

Conditioning on more observed children's C legitimately changes conditional
means and Schur covariances. Marginal compatibility does not itself establish
positive definiteness for every larger hypothetical family size. The fitter
checks the sizes represented in the data; it does not certify an infinite
exchangeable pedigree or unobserved family-size distributions.
